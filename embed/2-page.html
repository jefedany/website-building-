Good to Know

Questions, answered.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Elgin, IL and work throughout Chicagoland — the city of Chicago and the surrounding suburbs. Not sure if you're in our range? Just give us a call and we'll let you know.

Are estimates really free?

Yes — 100% free, with no obligation. We'll visit your home, talk through your project, and send a detailed written estimate. No pressure and no surprise fees later.

How much do projects cost?

Every project is priced individually after we see the space, but here are honest ballpark ranges to help set expectations:

  • Kitchen Remodeling$15,000–$75,000+

    Small refreshes on the low end; full gut remodels with layout changes push past $75k.

  • Bathroom Remodeling$12,000–$45,000+

    A basic same-layout remodel starts around $12k. Tile showers, custom glass, and plumbing moves run $20k–$45k+.

  • Basement Finishing$35,000–$150,000+

    An open basic finish starts around $35k. Add a bathroom, wet bar, or bedroom buildout and you're in the $95k–$160k+ range.

  • Drywall & Framing$500 / $4,500+

    Patch repairs start at $500. Full room framing, hang & finish starts at $4,500.

  • Interior Painting$650/room · $3–$7.50/sq ft

    Single-room minimum is $650. Whole-interior projects run $3–$7.50/sq ft depending on ceilings, trim, and prep.

  • Flooring$1,500+ · $8–$15/sq ft

    LVP/laminate with materials and install. Subfloor issues, stairs, and transitions add cost.

  • Tile Installation$1,500 backsplash · $14–$30/sq ft · $10,000+ showers

    Full tile showers — done with proper waterproofing — are the bigger investment.

  • Trim & Doors$1,500+ · $9–$16/linear ft

    Priced with material. Best bundled with other work rather than a small standalone run.

Final pricing depends on measurements, existing conditions, and material selections for each job. Your written estimate is fully itemized — these ranges just help set expectations up front.

Do you offer a warranty or guarantee?

Yes — both. Every job comes with a workmanship warranty (6 months standard; the term varies by job and can be custom-tailored to your project), plus a 100% customer-satisfaction guarantee. We're also licensed and insured with $1M in liability coverage, so if something isn't right, we make it right.

How soon can you start?

We're usually booked about two weeks out. Smaller jobs — like drywall patching, painting a room, or small plumbing work — can typically get started within two weeks. Larger remodels are scheduled a bit further ahead, so reach out early to claim your spot on the calendar.

Do I need to be home during the work?

No. Many of our clients give us access and go about their day. Our crews are licensed, insured, and respectful of your home — we protect your space, keep it clean, and lock up when we leave.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, cashier's check, Zelle, and credit card. For larger remodels, we'll set up a simple milestone payment schedule so it's clear and predictable.
